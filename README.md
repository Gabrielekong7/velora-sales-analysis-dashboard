# Velora Sales Distribution Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The Velora Sales Distribution Analysis Dashboard is an Excel-based data analysis project developed to analyze sales performance and identify important business trends.

The project uses Excel Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and an interactive dashboard to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard provides an overview of revenue, cost, profit, product performance, category performance, customer segments, sales channels, and monthly profit trends.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Calculate total revenue, cost, and profit.
- Identify the best-performing products.
- Analyze profit performance across different categories.
- Examine monthly profit trends.
- Compare customer segments.
- Compare sales channels.
- Create an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Profit Over Time

A monthly Pivot Table and line chart were created to analyze profit performance throughout the year.

The analysis shows that profit remained relatively stable during most months, with a significant increase toward the end of the year.

December recorded the highest monthly profit at approximately:

**$41,587.05**

---

### 2. Profit by Product

The analysis compared the profitability of five products:

- Product A
- Product B
- Product C
- Product D
- Product E

Product D was identified as the **top-performing product**, generating approximately:

**$124,532.57 in profit.**

Product E was the second-highest contributor with approximately:

**$50,333.50.**

---

### 3. Profit by Category

The dashboard compares profitability across categories including:

- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Tech

The category analysis helps identify which business areas contribute most to overall profitability.

---

### 4. Profit by Customer Segment

A Pivot Chart was used to compare profit across different customer segments.

This provides management with a better understanding of which customer groups generate the highest contribution to profit.

---

### 5. Profit by Sales Channel

The dashboard compares profit generated through different sales channels.

The analysis shows that one channel generated approximately:

**$146,289.47**

while the other generated approximately:

**$116,474.79.**

This comparison can help the business determine which channel performs better financially.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the analysis:

1. Total profit generated was approximately **$262,764.26**.

2. **Product D** was the strongest-performing product, contributing approximately **$124,532.57** in profit.

3. Profit remained fairly consistent across most months but increased significantly toward the end of the year.

4. **December recorded the highest monthly profit**, at approximately **$41,587.05**.

5. Product E was another strong contributor with approximately **$50,333.50** in profit.

6. Sales channels showed different levels of profitability, with one channel generating approximately **$146,289.47**.

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations can be considered:

- Increase focus on high-performing products such as Product D.
- Investigate the factors contributing to Product D's strong profitability.
- Analyze why December generated significantly higher profit.
- Strengthen the better-performing sales channel.
- Monitor low-performing products and categories.
- Use customer segment analysis to develop targeted marketing strategies.
- Continue monitoring monthly performance to identify seasonal trends.
